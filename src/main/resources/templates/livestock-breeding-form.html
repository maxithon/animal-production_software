You record what you observed — we calculate the rest

Enter the female, how and when she was bred, and who was involved. Dates like the expected due date and pregnancy-check date are calculated automatically from the animal's category, following FAO/ICAR livestock breeding record guidance — reducing manual entry and typing errors. Once she's been checked, confirm or record the outcome from this record's View page — that's a separate step from registering the breeding itself.
