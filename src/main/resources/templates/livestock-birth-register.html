When to use this form
Use this form when a mother already on your farm gives birth. The mother must already be registered in the system.
Animal arriving pregnant from outside?
Go to Register Livestock, set Acquisition Method = Purchase / Donation / Transfer, and tick "Currently Pregnant". The system will track the pregnancy automatically. Come back here to record the birth once she delivers on your farm.
